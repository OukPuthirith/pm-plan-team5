# Stakeholders: first list

The full list is in `stakeholders.csv` (12 rows, S01 sponsor, S02 project manager). Lab-11 will refine it into a stakeholder register and an engagement plan.

## Priority stakeholders
Two stakeholders have both high influence and high interest: the sponsor (S01, Head of the IT department) and the project manager (S02, the supervising lecturer). The sponsor funds the project, signs the charter and decides at the week-1, week-7 and week-13 gates whether to continue, change or stop, so the PM manages the sponsor first and closest, with a short status update every week and early warning of any change to scope, dates or the 200 USD cap. The PM is the person who turns the sponsor's expectations into a plan and a team routine, so the PM's weekly attention also goes to the three student rows (S03 to S05) and the lecturers who will run quizzes (S07), who are highly interested and have medium influence.

## Veto stakeholder
The data protection officer or registrar (S11) has low interest in the project but high influence, because the pilot cannot run in week 14 without approval to store student names and results, so the one-page data description must reach them before the week-13 gate.
